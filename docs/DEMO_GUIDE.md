# 3–5 Minute Sales Demonstration Guide
**Personalized Digital Business Solution**
**Prepared for:** SVCA Computer Training Academy & CSC Centre (West Bengal, India)
**Developed by:** Softrenix Technologies (https://www.softrenix.com)

---

## Overview & Demo Goal
This demonstration is designed to be presented to the academy director or business owner within **3 to 5 minutes** over a video meeting, phone conversation, or WhatsApp preview link.

The core objective is to showcase:
1. **Professional digital credibility** (a responsive, high-converting public website with instant lead capture and live announcement bar).
2. **Operational simplicity & multi-role collaboration** (switching between Owner, Receptionist, Instructor, and Accountant in 1 click).
3. **Dual-revenue stream financial clarity** (combining Student Course Installment Fees + CSC Citizen Service POS Billing, Expenses, and Cash Drawer Closing).
4. **Instant tangible outputs** (downloadable PDF fee receipts, CSC receipts, CR80 ID cards, A4 landscape certificates, student marksheets, and Excel reports).

---

## Step-by-Step 4-Minute Demonstration Script

### Minute 0:00 – 0:45: The Personalized Welcome & Public Website
- **Action:** Open the shared link at `/` (Welcome Page).
- **Pitch:**
  > *"We built this interactive demonstration specifically for your academy in West Bengal. It unifies your academic student batches, your CSC citizen services, staff operations, and financial cashbook under one simple system."*
- **Action:** Click **"View Public Website"** (navigates to `/website`).
- **Showcase:**
  - Dynamic top announcement banner (controlled directly from the management portal).
  - Course catalog (Basic Office, TallyPrime with GST, Graphic Design/DTP, ADCA, Programming).
  - Dedicated **CSC Citizen Helpdesk** section explaining citizen services.
  - Interactive **Admission Enquiry Form**.

### Minute 0:45 – 1:30: Live Website Lead to Student Conversion (Workflow 1 & 2)
- **Action:** Scroll to `/website#admission`.
- **Action:** Click **"Fill Sample Details"** and press **"Submit Sample Admission Enquiry"**.
- **Action:** Open the portal top bar and click **Enquiries** (`/portal/enquiries`).
- **Showcase:** The lead appeared instantly in the management pipeline.
- **Action:** Click **"Convert to Student"**, confirm batch and admission fee.
- **Pitch:**
  > *"When a prospective student reaches out, they never get lost in a WhatsApp chat. Your receptionist converts them into an enrolled student with an automated fee ledger in 5 seconds."*

### Minute 1:30 – 2:15: Daily Batch Attendance & Examination Marksheets (Workflow 3)
- **Action:** Go to **Attendance** (`/portal/attendance`). Select a batch, click **"Mark All Present"**, toggle one absent, click **"Save Attendance"**.
- **Action:** Navigate to **Examinations** (`/portal/examinations`). Select DCA Mid-Term, click **"Enter Marks"**, and click **"Download Marksheet PDF"**.
- **Pitch:**
  > *"No more thick registers. Attendance is marked in seconds, and when exams conclude, professional marksheets with letter grades are generated automatically."*

### Minute 2:15 – 3:00: CSC Counter POS Billing & Student Fee Collection (Workflow 4 & 5)
- **Action:** Navigate to **CSC Billing** (`/portal/csc-billing`).
- **Showcase:** Show the 2-panel POS interface. Click **PAN Card Application** and **Digital Caste Certificate**, enter customer name, and click **"Record Sale & Print Receipt"**.
- **Action:** Navigate to **Student Fees** (`/portal/billing`). Click **"Collect Payment"** on any student, collect ₹1,500 via UPI, and click **"Download A5 PDF Receipt"**.
- **Pitch:**
  > *"Your academy runs two revenue engines: student tuition and CSC citizen counter transactions. Our platform tracks both seamlessly with professional branded receipts."*

### Minute 3:00 – 3:45: Daily Cash Drawer Reconciliation & Reports (Workflow 6 & 7)
- **Action:** Go to **Daily Cashbook** (`/portal/cashbook`).
- **Showcase:** The drawer automatically tallies physical cash collections while keeping UPI digital transfers separated.
- **Action:** Show **"Close Cash Drawer"** workflow with discrepancy detection and closing summary PDF.
- **Action:** Go to **Reports** (`/portal/reports`) and click **"Export to Excel (.xlsx)"** on Fee Collections.
- **Pitch:**
  > *"At 7 PM when you close the centre, you know exactly how much physical cash is in your cash drawer, and your accountant can export full Excel workbooks with one click."*

### Minute 3:45 – 4:30: Role Switcher & Student Credentials (Closing Pitch)
- **Action:** Click the **Role Switcher** in the top bar to show how Receptionist or Instructor views only their authorized tools.
- **Action:** Quick preview of **Student ID Cards** (`/portal/id-cards`) and **Course Completion Certificates** (`/portal/certificates`).
- **Closing Pitch:**
  > *"This entire software can be deployed with your exact logo, courses, and staff workflow. Softrenix Technologies engineers solutions built specifically around how your business works. Let's schedule a 15-minute consultation to walk through your deployment."*
